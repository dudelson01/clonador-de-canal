=== DESCRIPTION ===
#Over60 #MuscleLoss #HealthyAging

After 60, many people slowly lose muscle without noticing, until getting up from a chair or climbing the stairs starts to feel harder. In this video, we look at one of the cheapest foods in the supermarket fridge: cottage cheese. One cup has about as much protein as four eggs.

You'll learn:
• why muscle loss (sarcopenia) speeds up after 60
• how much protein researchers recommend for older adults, and why breakfast matters
• 4 easy, cheap ways to eat cottage cheese in the morning
• who should be careful (salt, kidney disease)

Do you already eat cottage cheese for breakfast? Tell us in the comments.

=== DESCRIPTION ===
#StrongLegsAfter60 #ProteinAfter60 #Sarcopenia

After 60, your legs are often the first part of the body to quietly lose strength. The good news: you don't need expensive powders. In this video, we go through 7 cheap foods from an ordinary grocery list that can help your leg muscles, plus a simple 30-second test to check your leg strength at home.

The 7 foods: eggs, canned sardines, lentils and beans, Greek yogurt, milk, peanut butter and leafy greens. Number 7 isn't even a protein food, but in a study of 3,759 people it was linked to 11% stronger legs.

Which of these 7 do you already eat every week? Tell us in the comments.

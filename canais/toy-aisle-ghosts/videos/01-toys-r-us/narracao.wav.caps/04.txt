In nineteen ninety nine, the internet was exploding, and Toys"R"Us had a disaster on its hands. That Christmas, its website couldn't keep up with orders. So in two thousand, it made a choice that sounds unbelievable today.
It signed a ten year deal with Amazon. Toys"R"Us would become the exclusive toy seller on Amazon's site. In plain English: it handed its online store to the company that would one day eat the entire retail world.
For a few years, it worked. Then Amazon started letting other sellers list toys too. Toys"R"Us felt betrayed, and in two thousand four, it sued. In two thousand six, a judge let it walk away from the deal.
It won in court. But it lost something it could never get back: almost a decade of learning how to sell online. Years later, Amazon paid fifty one million dollars to settle the fight. By then, Amazon was the future. And Toys"R"Us was still figuring out its own website.

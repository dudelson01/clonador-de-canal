#ToysRUs #90sNostalgia #WhatHappenedTo

Do you remember walking through those doors for the first time, with the giant aisles, the video game cage and Geoffrey on every bag? For decades, Toys"R"Us was the place every kid in America dreamed about. Then, in 2018, it closed every store in the country.

In this video, we dig into the full rise and fall of Toys"R"Us: how Charles Lazarus turned a small baby-furniture shop into the biggest toy retailer on Earth, the deal with Amazon that backfired, the $6.6 billion buyout that buried the company in debt, and the final months before the lights went out. And what's left of the brand today.
